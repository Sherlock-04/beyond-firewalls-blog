# Beyond Firewalls: The Age of Autonomous Defense

> *When machines learn to attack and defend, who wins the cybersecurity arms race?*

An essay exploring the dynamics of machine-speed adversarial AI, the emerging arms race between automated attackers and autonomous defenders, and why enforceable boundaries are essential when AI becomes both the weapon and the shield.
